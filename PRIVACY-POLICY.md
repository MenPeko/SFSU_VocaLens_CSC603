# GlossTrans Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** April 8, 2026

GlossTrans ("we," "our," or "us") operates the GlossTrans Whiteboard Reader application
("App") on the MentraOS platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use,
and protect your information when you use our App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Data Processed During Use
- **Photos:** Images captured through your smart glasses when you press the capture button
- **Extracted Text:** Text recognized from captured images via optical character recognition (OCR)
- **Translations:** Translated versions of extracted text
- **Language Preference:** Your selected target translation language

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect your name, email address, or account credentials
- We do not collect location data
- We do not track browsing activity
- We do not use cookies or analytics trackers

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the translation service:
- Photos are sent to the Google Gemini API for text extraction and translation
- Translations are displayed on your device in real time
- Your language preference is stored in memory to persist during your session

## 3. Data Retention

- **Photos:** Not stored. Photos are sent directly to Google Gemini API for processing and are not saved on our servers.
- **Extracted Text and Translations:** Held in temporary memory only. All data is cleared when the application restarts or the session ends.
- **Language Preference:** Stored in memory during your active session only. Not persisted to disk.

No user data is written to a database or permanent storage by GlossTrans.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service to process your data:

**Google Gemini API**
- Purpose: Optical character recognition (OCR) and language translation
- Data sent: Captured photos and extracted text
- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google's Generative AI terms: https://ai.google.dev/terms

**MentraOS Platform**
- Purpose: Device connectivity, session management, and app delivery
- MentraOS manages the connection between your smart glasses and this App
- Refer to MentraOS's privacy policy for details on platform-level data handling

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
Your data is shared only with Google Gemini API as described above, solely
for the purpose of providing the translation service.

## 6. Data Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your data:
- All API communications use HTTPS encryption
- No user data is persisted to disk or databases
- Session data is held in memory and cleared on termination
- Photos are processed and immediately discarded

## 7. Your Rights Under California Law (CCPA/CPRA)

As a California resident, you have the right to:
- **Know** what personal information is collected and how it is used
- **Delete** your personal information (note: GlossTrans does not persist data beyond your session)
- **Opt-out** of the sale of personal information (GlossTrans does not sell your data)
- **Non-discrimination** for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@glosstrans.com.

## 8. Children's Privacy

GlossTrans is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not
knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child
has used our App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 9. Photos and Sensitive Content

Photos captured through GlossTrans may inadvertently contain sensitive or
personally identifiable information visible on whiteboards, signs, or
documents. Users are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate
permission to capture and process such content. GlossTrans does not store
or review captured images.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of
the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
